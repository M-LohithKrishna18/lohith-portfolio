Love

Before I could understand business, I understood a restaurant. The smell of a kitchen before service. The way a room fills up. What it feels like when something you made makes someone happy.

My family ran Social Monkey in Hyderabad. It didn't survive. But something about standing in that space as a kid — watching, absorbing, belonging to it — never really left.

I cook. Not as a hobby. More like a need.

Social Monkey, Hyderabad — 2017. I was ten.

The food I keep coming back to:

  • Hyderabadi Biryani — no explanation needed
  • Bagara rice with chicken and mutton curry
  • Coconut rice with chicken

Andhra food is not something I learned from a menu. It's something I know from a kitchen. That difference matters to me — and it's why I want to build in this space someday.

Cricket

I am very emotionally committed to cricket. I've been watching it, analysing it, arguing about it for as long as I can remember. It's not a hobby — it's a language I think in.

The Ice · Mumbai Indians

The Conqueror

Rohit Sharma — The calm executioner

"Excellence is not a singular act, but a habit." — Aristotle

Mumbai Indians taught me the art of the conqueror. The calm executioner. Effortless elegance and the relentless habit of winning. They are the proof that glory belongs to those who make winning a routine — not a moment.

The Fire · Royal Challengers Bengaluru

The Passion

"There is a stubbornness about me that never can bear to be frightened at the will of others. My courage always rises at every attempt to intimidate me." — Jane Austen

Royal Challengers Bengaluru taught me the art of passion. The heartbeat. Pure, unadulterated emotion. To love RCB is to understand that true loyalty doesn't need a trophy to validate it.
